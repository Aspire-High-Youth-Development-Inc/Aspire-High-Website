Our Programs

Find the opportunity that fits where you are in your education and career.

Work Study

Learn while you earn! Through Aspire High’s Federal Work-Study Program, eligible college students can gain hands-on experience in areas such as Information Technology (IT), Human Resources (HR), data analytics, operations, administration, and more.

Learn more about Work Study →

Internship

Build your resume with hands-on experience. Our internship program provides students and emerging professionals with hands-on experience in a mission-driven nonprofit environment. Work directly with our youth programs and see the immediate impact of your work.

CPT

Apply what you’re learning in class to the real world! Aspire High offers Curricular Practical Training (CPT) opportunities for eligible international students seeking practical experience related to their academic field of study.

Learn more about CPT →

OPT

Kickstart your career with purpose! Aspire High welcomes eligible international graduates seeking to gain professional experience through Optional Practical Training (OPT). Gain valuable post-grad experience in the non-profit sector while helping our students thrive.

Learn more about OPT →
