Why partner with us?

Partnering with Aspire High allows us to provide innovative one-on-one group mentoring to low-income and first-generation youth.

Prepared for the future

Aspire High is focused on preparing young people to make informed decisions and successfully meet the challenges of the 21st-century global workplace.

Mentoring on your campus

We provide an opportunity for you and your organization to mentor youth directly on your campus, allowing them to connect with your organization and helping prepare them for the future.

Our mentors

Our mentors are volunteers from your organization who play a critical role in our events. They provide one-on-one group mentoring during our events, fostering the academic, social, and emotional growth of their mentees.
