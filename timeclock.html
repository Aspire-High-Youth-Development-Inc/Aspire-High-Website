Employee Time Clock

Clock in and out with your name and your private Employee ID. Times are recorded by our server for HR.

1Sign In

Enter your name and your Employee ID.
Your Employee ID works like a password, so never share it. Forgot it? Contact HR.

2Clock Action

Select the action you are performing. Use a break to step away without clocking out.
Working 6 or more hours? You must take a 30-minute meal break: choose Start Break → Meal break, then End Break when you're back.

3Notes (Optional)

Add any additional notes (special circumstances, location, etc.).

Missed Punch / Correction Request

Report a missed clock-in/out or request a correction. HR will review it.
Keep your Employee ID privateIt proves who you are. Clocking in for someone else is not allowed.
