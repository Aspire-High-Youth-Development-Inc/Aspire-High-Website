College Partnership & Event Planning Form

A simple planning form for college student representatives, campus departments, clubs, chapters, and partner organizations. Tell us who you are, review and agree to your tasks and duties, name who is responsible for each one, and confirm the details needed for a successful event.

How to complete this form
Complete the required fields marked *. Use the dropdowns where available. If your university, organization, or program is not listed, choose Other and type it in the box that appears. For anything not decided yet, select Pending. Please name a person for each host task so everyone knows who is following up.

Hosting Organization Agreement: your tasks and duties *

By checking the box below, your organization agrees to these duties. You will name the person responsible for each one in Section 4.

  1. Book 3 conference rooms. Two for workshops and one for the large group, where everyone will also eat lunch.
  2. Obtain university paras. Work with Admissions, Student Organizations, Athletics and Community Engagement to get paras for the youth.
  3. Recruit mentors and volunteers. Recruit from clubs and organizations that give back to the community: speakers, mentors, photo/video helpers, and department representatives who will talk to the youth about their services during lunch.
  4. Provide food for lunch. Obtain sponsorships from other organizations, restaurants or the university cafeteria for the volunteers and youth. We usually get pizza, water and a snack.
  5. Facilitate the event and workshops. Assign 2 facilitators per workshop (8 in total) plus a host who keeps everyone on schedule. Facilitators do not have to be sorority members.
  6. Schedule and attend 3 virtual planning meetings with the Aspire High College Coordinator: orientation with all hosting organizations (2nd week of August), one-on-one (3rd week of September), and a final check-in (3rd week of October).
Program schedule: The program starts at 9:00 AM and ends at 2:30 PM. You will confirm this when you sign in Section 7.
