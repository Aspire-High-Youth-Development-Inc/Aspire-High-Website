How Aspire High’s tutoring model works

At Aspire High Youth Development Inc., we strive to expand our students’ knowledge beyond the barriers through our tutoring sessions. Homework shouldn’t be an obstacle, but rather an opportunity for you to grow.

Our mission is to foster a lighter yet still structured guidance through our tutoring sessions so that students can learn the most valuable skills of time management, goal-setting, and fruitful knowledge acquired through the teachings of our well-trusted tutors.

Our programs

Tutors and mentors support students across all of these programs.

Academic and Mental Health Support

We help our students learn leadership skills, assist with application processes, and help apply for financial aid and scholarships.

Time Management and Academic Planning

We help students stay organized and accountable through personalized calendars, assignment tracking, and time management strategies. With regular check-ins and clear communication between students, parents, teachers, and tutors, we ensure consistent support and steady academic progress.

Senior Specific Service

We support high school seniors through every step of the transition after graduation, from college applications, financial aid, and scholarships to building resumes and mastering interviews. With personalized guidance, we help seniors explore career paths, clarify their goals, and confidently take their next steps.

Franklin Tutoring Program

High school and middle school tutoring sessions that take place via Zoom, where tutors meet and teach the student directly from the school-designed materials. Students prepare for exams and build study techniques and time management strategies.

Scholar Athletes HS Junior & Senior Program

Student athlete–focused programs that guide and teach students to balance their sports life and their academic goals as high school upperclassmen.

K-12 Academic Consulting Program

This tutoring program focuses on the overall academic development of students, from kindergarten through 12th grade, to ensure a consistent and purposeful, goal-oriented path for students’ future career plans.
